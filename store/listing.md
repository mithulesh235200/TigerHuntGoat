## Full description
Aadu Puli Aatam (Lambs & Tigers) is the classic Tamil asymmetric strategy board
game, beautifully rebuilt for mobile.

**HOW TO PLAY**
- 🐐 Goats (15): place one per turn, then move along lines. Win by surrounding
  and trapping all 3 tigers so they cannot move.
- 🐯 Tigers (3): slide along lines and capture by jumping over goats, like
  checkers. Win by capturing 5 goats.

**FEATURES**
- Play vs a minimax AI with 3 difficulty levels, local 2-player hotseat, or
  online realtime rooms with invite links.
- Fully offline PWA: install once, play anywhere — no internet needed for
  vs-AI and 2-player modes.
- 6 app themes including Ivory Day light mode, full Tamil language mode,
  interactive tutorial, match replay with shareable links, enforced turn
  clock, tactical coach, and career analytics.
- Realistic tiger & goat artwork, haptics, sounds, and keyboard shortcuts.

Traditional strategy, modern craft. Trap the hunt!
